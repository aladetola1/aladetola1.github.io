From Stable Placement to Grasp-Aware Placement

A simulation-based visualization of grasp-aware robotic placement using a Robot-Independent Geometric Manipulation Proxy (RGMP) within a Quality-Diversity framework.

RGMP robotic placement simulation

This PyBullet simulation visualization illustrates a robotic manipulation sequence associated with the RGMP research framework, where stable object placements are evaluated according to geometric grasp-friendliness for subsequent manipulation.

Research context: This work was conducted at Sorbonne Université – ISIR within the ASIMOV team. The research combines geometric modeling, robotic simulation, Quality-Diversity optimization, and experimental validation.
